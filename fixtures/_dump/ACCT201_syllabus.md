**Course Objectives**

This course is the first of two accounting principles courses. These courses are designed to be first-year courses taken in the business program. The courses are intended to introduce students to the basics of accounting, first from an external perspective (ACCT 201) and then from an internal perspective (ACCT 202).

The goal of this course is to introduce you to accounting principles and how we apply them to create financial statements. In this course, we will explore the following questions:

*   How do we talk about accounting and financial concepts?
*   What are the four basic financial statements, how are they created, and how are they used?
*   What is the accounting cycle, and how do various transactions affect the parts of this cycle?

**Description of Instructional Methodology**

**_Class Sessions_**

Before a class in which we start a new chapter, students will be expected to have read the chapter via the SmartBook Assignment. Class time may include some lecture over the reading material and work-out of applicable problems. However, not all items in the reading will be covered, though you will be expected to have a basic understanding of those items. The remaining class time will be spent discussing the material and its application. You are expected to participate actively with the discussion and complete the problems before I work them on the board.

**_Your Behavior_**

Professional etiquette is expected, both in and out of class. Your conduct is expected to demonstrate respect for and garner respect from your colleagues. Be ready to actively engage in classroom activities on-time and with adequate preparation. Cell phones should be silenced and should not be used during class, and distracting materials (e.g. laptops, headphones, and other electronic devices not used for class purposes) put away.

**_Homework and SmartBook Assignments_**

Assignments will be completed via Connect. There will be two types of assignments completed in Connect: homework and SmartBook (SB) assignments.

**_SB Assignments_**

SB assignments are based on completeness rather than correctness and will generally be due on the first day we start a new chapter. Each SB assignment will be worth 10 points for a total of 100 points. I will drop the SB assignment on which you score the lowest.

**_Homework_**

Homework assignments will be due when we finish a chapter. Homework assignments will be worth a total of 200 points with individual assignments being worth variable amounts depending on length and importance. Chapters 1-3 are foundational, and as such are both worth more points and are mandatory. As long as you receive at least 75 % of the points on a homework assignment, I will round your grade up to 100% for that assignment. At the end of the semester, I will drop either one 20 pt homework assignment or two 10 pt homework assignments.

**_A Note on Due Dates_**

There will be no makeups or extensions for SmartBook or homework assignments. **Pay attention to due dates and make sure you turn things in on time.**

**IT IS VERY DIFFICULT TO PASS THIS CLASS IF YOU DO NOT COMPLETE THE SMARTBOOK AND HOMEWORK ASSIGNMENTS. IF YOU CHOOSE TO FORGO THESE POINT OPPORTUNITIES, THE HIGHEST GRADE YOU CAN EARN IS A 70% (C).**

**_Lab_**

Labs are extra sessions given during the week intended to provide you extra help with the material and to prepare you for that week’s homework and/or test. Lab attendance is not strictly required, and therefore, lab attendance points will be allocated either based on the percentage of lab sessions you attend or the percentage of final exam points you earn, whichever is higher. **In other words, if you choose not to attend the lab sessions, the final exam will be worth 300 points instead of 200 points.**

**IF YOU ARE UNABLE TO ATTEND THE SELECTED LAB TIME DUE TO ANOTHER CLASS OR ATHLETIC CONFLICTS, PLEASE COME TALK TO ME. THERE ARE OTHER TUTORING OPTIONS AVAILABLE THAT I WOULD BE WILLING TO COUNT TOWARDS YOUR LAB GRADE.**

**_Exams_**

We will have 3 exams during the semester, each focusing on 2-3 chapters. Each exam will be worth 100 points for a total of 300 points. Exams are based on the material in the textbook, assigned problems on homework assignments and assessments, and class lectures. You will be allowed a single, hand-written formula sheet and a calculator (not a cell phone!).

**_Final Exam_**

The final exam will be a comprehensive exam covering all of the material from the course. It will be worth 200 points. Exams may consist of problem and concept questions in a matching, multiple choice, fill-in-the-blank, and problem format.

**If you are unable to take the exam on the scheduled date, you must notify me in advance. I reserve the right to raise the difficulty level of the make-up exam.**
